## Project Description
Botheo Matlala state that, Botheo’s Baker & Sweets is a small local business based in 
Sekhukhune Lekgoareng owned by a female who is driven by a passion of baking and desire to 
meet the growing demand for homemade treats such as teaching how to bake, sell cakes and 
muffins. The business was established in 2023 to provide fresh, affordable and high-quality 
baked goods to its surrounding community at large. The organisation focuses on using simple 
and quality ingredients and maintaining consistent taste. Despite not having any formal online 
website, the business continues growing rapidly, satisfying customers and committed deliver 
fresh products within the area. She teaches basic baking techniques in which her training is 
mostly practical, allowing learners to gain hands-on experience. This approach boost 
confidence and develop entrepreneur skills by empowering others to have potential in starting 
their own baking business and economic growth in the community. 
Mission: 
Customer service is a prior to their hearts. 
Vision: 
To stay consistence and provide best service in the community. 
Target audience: 
Birthdays, Weddings and any other celebrating events.
